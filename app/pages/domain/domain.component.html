What is a Domain?

A domain is a public name mapped to an IP address, used to identify you publicly online (e.g., instead of a raw IP like 123.55.**.*9). Anyone can access it via any web browser.

Nameservers & Domain Activation

What Does "Private" Mean for Your Domain?

In the context of AfroGift domains, "private" means masking your local IP address with our nameservers. Every user connected to the internet has a local IP address (visible by running npm run ip in any AfroGift App). We provide a public identity without exposing your personal or private IP address.

Important Connectivity Notice

VPN & Router Connectivity: If you activate your domain connected to a VPN or router and the VPN server goes down, you'll need to update your nameserver record before receiving trades until your recorded IP responds online.

This can affect active traders who suddenly stop receiving trades. The solution is often simply updating your Gift Card domain Nameserver.
